## 1.0.0

- Initial stable release of `flutter_smooth_marquee`.
- Smart auto-overflow detection: measures child bounds and remains static with zero CPU/GPU overhead when content fits within available width.
- Support for arbitrary rich widgets, icons, inline spans, and custom layouts.
- Hardware-accelerated alpha gradient edge masks with customizable fade width.
- Interactive pause controls for pointer hover and touch events.
- Bi-directional scrolling (`forward` and `reverse`) with customizable velocity.
- `SmoothMarqueeText` drop-in widget for effortless typography tickers.
- 100% test coverage across unit and widget behavior.
